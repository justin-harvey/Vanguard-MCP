What just happened

The model never saw a row: a named, pre-written query ran on a read-only connection, the integrity checks confirmed the result is internally consistent (so it may be presented as fact), and the findings surface data conditions worth a human's attention, like grant cash drawn ahead of spending. The result hash lets an auditor recompute and compare; the audit entry is appended to a hash-chained log, so Verify the audit chain reports the first entry that was ever altered. Tamper-evident, not an assertion.
